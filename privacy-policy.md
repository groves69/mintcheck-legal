# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 21, 2026

MintCheck ("MintCheck," "we," "us," or "our") provides an app that gives you an
automated, informational pre-grade estimate for trading cards. This Privacy
Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices
you have.

## Information We Collect

- **Account information.** When you create an account, we collect the
  username, email address, and password you provide (handled via Firebase
  Authentication). We never store your password in plain text.
- **Card images.** Photos of your cards that you capture or select for
  grading are uploaded so the app can analyze them. If you choose to save a
  result to your history, those images and the resulting scores are stored
  under your account.
- **Grading history.** Pre-grade scores, categories, and notes you save are
  stored so you can view your past results.
- **Subscription and purchase information.** If you subscribe to Bundle
  Grading, your purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play,
  with subscription status managed through RevenueCat. **We do not collect or
  store your payment card information** — that is handled entirely by Apple
  or Google.
- **Basic usage and device information.** Standard technical information
  (such as crash logs and basic device/OS details) may be collected
  automatically to help us keep the app working correctly.

## Support Requests

When you contact support through the app, we collect the subject, message,
and category you provide, along with basic device information (app
version, OS, and device model) that is automatically included to help us
diagnose and resolve your request. This information is sent to our support
email address and used only to respond to you.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

- Provide and operate the pre-grade estimate features (including Detailed
  Analysis, which sends your card photos to our backend and to Google's
  Gemini API for processing)
- Maintain your account and grading history
- Manage your subscription and verify purchase status
- Diagnose bugs and improve the app

We do not sell your personal information.

## Third-Party Services

We rely on the following third-party services, each governed by its own
privacy policy:

- **Firebase (Google)** — authentication, database, and image storage
- **Google Gemini API** — image analysis for Detailed Analysis mode
- **RevenueCat** — subscription status management
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** — payment processing for subscriptions

## Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored using Firebase's infrastructure with industry-standard
security practices. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100%
secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account data and saved grading history until you delete your
account or request deletion. To request deletion of your account and
associated data, contact us at support.mintcheck@gmail.com.

## Children's Privacy

MintCheck is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly
collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or
delete your personal information. Contact us at support.mintcheck@gmail.com to
exercise these rights.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the
effective date above when we do.

## Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact us at support.mintcheck@gmail.com.
