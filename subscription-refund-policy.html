Effective Date: [Effective Date]

This policy explains how MintCheck's paid subscription (Bundle Grading) works, including billing, cancellation, and refunds.

The Subscription

Bundle Grading is a recurring subscription that unlocks batch grading of multiple cards in a single session, among other features described in the app at the time of purchase.

Billing & Auto-Renewal

Payment is processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play. MintCheck does not collect or store your payment card information — that is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

Your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period, at the price you agreed to at purchase (subject to the "Price Changes" section below).

Free Trials

If a free trial is offered, its length and terms will be disclosed at the time of purchase. Unless canceled before the trial ends, your subscription will automatically convert to a paid subscription and you will be charged.

Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings — MintCheck cannot cancel it for you directly. Canceling stops future renewals; you'll continue to have access through the end of the period you already paid for.

Refunds

Because payment is processed by Apple or Google, refund requests are handled according to their respective policies:

  • Apple App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund policy

MintCheck does not directly process or guarantee refunds for subscription charges.

Price Changes

If we change subscription pricing, we will provide notice as required by Apple's or Google's respective policies before any new price takes effect for existing subscribers.
